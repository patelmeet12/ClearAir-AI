# ClearAir AI — Product Overview

## Mission & Problem Statement

Urban air pollution, particularly winter temperature inversion smog, is an urgent global public health crisis. Trapped particulates (PM2.5, PM10) and toxic gases (NO2, SO2, O3) penetrate deep into human lung tissue and enter the bloodstream, causing acute respiratory distress, pediatric asthma spikes, and cardiovascular events.

Standard weather apps present raw numbers (e.g., "AQI 340") without practical context. Families are left asking:
- *Can my child participate in outdoor recess today?*
- *When is it safe to ventilate our home?*
- *How can I protect my elderly parents without spending thousands on commercial equipment?*
- *How can I adapt my commute so I don't breathe concentrated exhaust while idling in traffic?*

**ClearAir AI** bridges the gap between atmospheric science and daily family life. It translates live air quality telemetry into prioritized, evidence-based, low-cost actions, empowering users to breathe cleaner air while simultaneously lowering their own urban emissions footprint.

---

## Target Audience Personas

1. **Urban Parents**: Need clear, non-alarmist guidance on school recess, playground visits, pediatric N95 mask fit, and clean-air bedroom zones.
2. **Caregivers of Vulnerable Individuals**: Need proactive scheduling to keep asthmatic children and seniors indoors during morning and evening smog peaks.
3. **Daily Urban Commuters**: Looking for optimal transit timing, vehicle HVAC recirculation tactics, and anti-idling habits to cut personal exposure and fuel expenses.
4. **Budget-Conscious Renters & Homeowners**: Want high-impact, low-cost DIY air purification (MERV-13 box fans, weatherstripping, damp mopping) rather than expensive proprietary systems.
5. **Community Clean Air Champions**: Seeking collaborative challenges to encourage neighborhood anti-idling and collective transit adoption.
